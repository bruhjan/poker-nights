Privacy

Poker Nights. Updated 14 October 2026.

Poker Nights is a small app for tracking buy-ins and chip counts at a home poker game. It is run by Rajan Rathi for friends.

What is stored

  • Group names, player names (as typed in), buy-ins, chip counts and the times they were entered.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, your email address and the list of groups you have opened, so they come back on any phone you sign in on.

What is not stored

No passwords, payment details, contacts or location. Google sign-in only shares your basic profile (name and email) with the app.

Where it lives

Data is kept in a Supabase database. The app's pages are served by GitHub Pages. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything other than running the app.

Who can see it

A group is visible only to people who have its link. Anyone with a group's edit link can change that group. Your saved list of groups is visible only to you.

Deleting your data

Email rajanrathi259@gmail.com to have your account, or a group, deleted.
